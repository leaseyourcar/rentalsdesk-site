How it works

  1. 1You send us your routine requests. Maintenance requests reach us by email, set up around how they come in today.
  2. 2We chase the vendor, the owner and the tenant. We book your existing vendors, ask owners for approvals, and keep tenants updated in writing until the job is done.
  3. 3You get a report by 7 AM your time. Every business day, one short email shows what moved and what needs you.

What happens to each request

  • It reaches us. A routine request comes in by email.
  • The tenant hears from us within 1 business hour.
  • We contact a vendor the same business day. Requests after 4 PM PT: by 10 AM PT the next business day.
  • We chase until it's settled. We confirm the appointment, ask the owner for approval and send reminders, rebook a vendor who doesn't show, and tell the tenant what's happening.
  • It shows up in your morning report until it's closed, with anything that needs your decision at the top.
  • Emergencies skip the line. If an emergency reaches us, we pass it straight to you.

Business hours are Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Pacific.

$299 founding pilot: the terms

What you get. For 30 days, Rentals Desk coordinates up to 30 routine maintenance requests for your properties, by email. You get a report by 7 AM in your local time every business day. The pilot costs $299, paid up front. There's no setup fee and no annual contract.

Your 30 days start on the first day we begin handling your requests, not on the day you pay.

What stays with you. Your team retains control of emergencies, approval decisions, and payments. We coordinate approvals, which means we ask owners and chase them for an answer, but we never make the decision ourselves. If an emergency reaches us, we pass it straight to you.

Our response standard. When a routine request reaches us, we let the tenant know we've got it within 1 business hour, and we contact a vendor the same business day (requests after 4 PM PT: by 10 AM PT the next business day). Business hours are Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Pacific, not counting US federal holidays. If a request comes in outside business hours, the hour starts at 8 AM the next business day.

What counts as one request. One request is one maintenance issue at one unit, from the time it reaches us until it's closed. If the same issue comes back within 14 days, it's still the same request. A new issue is a new request.

If you get busy. We email you when you reach your 25th request. Once you've reached 30, every new request goes straight back to you, along with a written note saying Rentals Desk is not handling it, so nothing falls through the cracks. Requests we're already working on keep getting follow-up through day 30. We'll only take on more if you approve it in writing, and you'll never get a surprise charge.

Our guarantee. You get your full $299 back if, during the pilot, either of these happens:

  • We miss the response standard on 3 or more requests.
  • We miss 2 or more morning reports (due by 7 AM your time).

One late reply on its own doesn't count as a failure.
