How the pilot works

What you send us: your vendor list, your approval limits (for example, "anything over $350 needs me"), and owner contact details.

How requests reach us: set up an auto-forward rule for the maintenance notification emails from your property software (for example AppFolio, Buildium, or Rent Manager). Then forward or cc your maintenance inbox. Your tenants don't have to change anything.

  • Days 1-5, setup: we load your vendors and rules, test the forwarding, and send you a sample brief.
  • Weeks 2-4, live: we coordinate routine requests and send the 7 AM brief every business day.
  • Day 30, review: we go over response times, what got done, and what needed you. You decide whether to continue month-to-month at $999/month.

How you pay: after we confirm your properties are a fit, we reply with a secure Stripe payment link. You pay your first month, $999, up front for the 30-day pilot. After that it's $999/month, billed monthly, and you can cancel anytime.

How we keep you in control

  • You approve anything over your dollar limit.
