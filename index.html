What we handle

  • Intake: we log every routine maintenance request that reaches our inbox.
  • Vendor scheduling: we contact your vendors, book a time, and get the booking confirmed.
  • Owner approvals: we send the approval request and follow up until we have an answer.
  • Status updates: we tell the tenant and your team where each request stands.
  • Morning summary: a 7 AM email showing what moved yesterday and what needs your decision.

What we don't handle

Emergencies, legal notices, payments, or any financial decision. Those stay with your team. If a request looks urgent, we send it straight to you and do nothing else with it.

Coming later: rent reminders and vendor insurance (COI) tracking.
